ℹ️ About IUST Transit Portal

🎯 Purpose & Mission

The IUST Transit Portal was developed to eliminate the daily uncertainty of bus transit for both students and university officials/faculty. By providing live satellite telemetry, congestion warnings, and accurate morning inbound ETAs, the system streamlines campus commuting.
