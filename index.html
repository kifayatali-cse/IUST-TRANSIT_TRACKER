About IUST Transit Portal

Official Intelligent Transit Telemetry System

🎯 Why Was This Built?

The IUST Transit Portal was engineered to solve daily commute challenges faced by thousands of students and faculty traveling across Kashmir to the Islamic University of Science & Technology, Awantipora. Traditional bus operations suffered from unpredictable morning delays, harsh winter weather disruptions, and lack of real-time coordinate broadcasting.

This system bridges the communication gap between university drivers and commuters, eliminating roadside waiting times and providing high-accuracy morning ETAs to the campus gate.

⚙️ Key System Capabilities

  • Live GPS Telemetry: Real-time coordinate synchronization via Firebase Realtime Database.
  • Morning Inbound Radar: Time-gated ETA algorithms calibrated for morning commutes (07:30 AM – 10:00 AM).
  • Proximity Alerts: Instant audio alerts when your bus is within 1 km of your location.
  • Security Gateway: Single active device session controls and brute-force prevention.
